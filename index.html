Phase I — Archangel: Genesis

Seed:

The Path to Archangel Cosmos

    How to Play

    • You govern Planet Elysia and its 100,000 Angelic Humans.
    • Pick a structure in the Build tab, then tap a tile on the map. Tap a building with nothing selected to inspect / demolish.
    • Each tick is one day. Structures produce and consume resources. Population grows when there is food, water, housing and energy.
    • Sell surplus in the Market: prices rise when stock is scarce and fall when you flood the market.
    • Follow Missions and the Archangel AI advisor. Reach the Moon to finish Genesis (about an hour).
    • Host tab: the Royal Heavenly Host council (decrees, sector governors) and diplomacy with the Lumarians, Aethari, Titanborn and Celestians as you meet them.
    • After the Moon colony, Phase 2 Starborn opens: press 🪐 for the Helion solar system, build ships and colonise worlds.
    • Phase 3 Galactic Empires (🌌) follows Starborn. The Corps tab runs your corporation and alliances; the AI tab answers questions and writes your Chronicle.
    • In 3D: drag to rotate, right-drag or two fingers to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom. Zoom with the mouse wheel, pinch, or the ＋/－ buttons; drag to pan.
    • The game autosaves every 30 days; you can also Save/Load manually.

    Saves are stored in this browser only (localStorage). Clearing browser data erases them.
